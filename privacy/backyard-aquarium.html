Backyard Aquarium: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Backyard Aquarium is a game by Crashbell (developer account "Duy Nguyen An" on Google Play). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no account or sign-in, no online leaderboard, no advertising, and no analytics service that sends data off your device.

Information stored on your device

Your pond, fish, fish book, settings and progress are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game may show a local reminder notification. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no information is sent anywhere.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising or tracking libraries. Google Play, which you use to download the game, has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version starts handling personal information (for example ads or analytics), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data safety section will say so.
